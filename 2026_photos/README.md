# 2026 photos

Photos for the 2026 Extra Life Nerds 24-hour gaming FUNdraiser (Game Day,
November 14), kept in one place so the site, emails and anyone else can grab
them. Taken by the owner on setup night, 2026-11-13, between 5:36 and 7:01 PM,
at the venue (Improving, Lakeside Center, 3033 Excelsior Blvd #180,
Minneapolis).

**How they were saved (2026-11-14):** the phone originals are 4000×3000.
These copies are scaled to 2400px on the long side (JPEG quality 84), turned
upright, and stripped of all camera metadata (model, timestamps, location
fields). That is plenty for the web and email; for print, ask the owner for the
originals. Nothing on the site uses them yet.

Like `2026_sponsors/`, this folder is the source. The site imports what it needs from
here, and emails need their own copies under `public/email/assets/`, since an
email can only show an image from a fixed public address.

## prizes/: the prize tables

`table-1` to `table-6` run left to right along the window. Each group of prizes
has a $25 Source Comics & Games gift certificate and a 3D-printed "10" token in
front of it. Games visible include Schitt's Creek *Love That Journey*, *Fort*,
*Neighborhood Hide & Seek*, *Ahoy*, *Biomos*, *Distant Suns*, *Mystic Paths*,
*Codenames*, *Codenames Duet*, *Eternal Palace*, *Wispwood*, *Get 9*,
*Kingyo* and the Extra Life *Puzzles of Color* puzzle, plus Squishmallows and
other plush.
