# Email archive

Every campaign that has been sent, one folder per send, named
`YYYY-MM-<slug>` by the month it went out. Each folder holds the exact HTML
that was pasted into Brevo (plus any variants, like a VIP version). Nothing
here is edited after the fact; it is the record of what people received.

Each archived email also stays viewable on the site at
`https://elnerds.com/email/archive/<folder>/`, served from
`public/email/archive/<folder>/index.html`.

## Archiving a campaign

As soon as a campaign is sent:

1. `git mv` the campaign file(s) from `email/` into a new
   `email/archive/YYYY-MM-<slug>/` folder.
2. Copy `public/email/index.html` to `public/email/archive/YYYY-MM-<slug>/index.html`
   so the email keeps a permanent URL.
3. Add a row to the table above.

`https://elnerds.com/email/template/` shows the template (generated by
`branding/email/build-preview.py`). `https://elnerds.com/email/current/` shows
the campaign under review (`public/email/current/index.html`, a copy of the
file in `email/`); once that campaign is archived, replace it with the next
draft or a redirect stub to its archive page. `https://elnerds.com/email/`
itself is a small index page (`public/email/index.html`) linking to all three.
