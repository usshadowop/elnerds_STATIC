Don’t forget: bring an HDMI cable to connect to the monitors.

Getting here

Park in the lot off Excelsior Blvd

Improving is inside Lakeside Center, 3033 Excelsior Blvd #180, Minneapolis. Park in the big lot beside the building, off Excelsior Blvd.

■  Red: coming from the east on W Lake St
■  Blue: coming from the west on W Lake St, or the south on Excelsior Blvd
The way in
1.  Head for the covered Lakeside Center entrance.
2.  Just inside, find our Game Day arrows on the directory.
3.  Walk down the hallway and take the stairs up one level, or the elevators around the corner to the left.
4.  Improving is suite 180 on the first floor.

Win while you play

Prize drawings & silent auctions

The prize tables are set up and waiting, and every ticket and bid helps the kids.

Prize drawings: 9 drawings, 10 AM to 10 PM
•  $1 per ticket.
•  A drawing every 1½ hours: 10 AM, 11:30 AM, 1 PM, 2:30 PM, 4 PM, 5:30 PM, 7 PM, 8:30 PM and 10 PM.
•  Multiple prizes in every drawing, and every ticket you enter can win in any of them.
•  Every raffle prize comes with a $10 donation token (more on those below).
•  Free tickets: enter any Game Day event and get 1. Event winners get 5 more.
•  You must be present to win.
Silent auctions: all event long
•  Running through the entire event, 8 AM Saturday to 8 AM Sunday.
•  You don’t need to be present to win.
